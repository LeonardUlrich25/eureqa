Our mission

A monthly injection of the wonderful, the inspiring and the hopeful.

The news is a continuous stream of disheartening information. eureqa! is the antidote: every month we highlight the work of student entrepreneurial and artistic ventures from universities across London, Oxford and Cambridge, celebrating innovation and inviting collaboration.

We don't publish pitch decks. We publish the messy middle: the pivots, the chased drones, the forgotten lyrics, the rebrands that worked. Real insights from the people actually building, told in their own words.
