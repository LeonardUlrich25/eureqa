Just the email address (and name, if you choose to give one) you provide when you subscribe to the newsletter. This website needs no account, sets no tracking cookies and runs no analytics.

Solely to send you the monthly eureqa! edition and occasional eureqa! updates, such as event announcements, so exactly what you signed up for and nothing else. Nobody, for their own purposes — we do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties. 5. How long we keep it

For as long as you're subscribed. If you unsubscribe, you're removed from the mailing list; if the project ever winds down, the list is deleted.

Under UK GDPR you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, restriction of or objection to its processing, and data portability, and you can withdraw consent at any time, just email us.
